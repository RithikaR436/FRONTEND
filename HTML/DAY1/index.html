A girl joined an IT training institute on her first day. Everyone spoke English, and she felt nervous. She quietly thought, "Can I do this?" The trainer smiled and said, "No one came here as an expert. Everyone started as a beginner." Those words gave her confidence. She practiced English every day, learned coding step by step, and never gave up. A few months later, she cleared an interview and got a job at an IT company. As she walked out holding her offer letter, she smiled and thought, "The day I stopped being afraid and started trying was the day my life changed."
