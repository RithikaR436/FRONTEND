Customer Feedback - Food Delivery Apps

Food delivery platforms like Swiggy, Zomato, and Zepto receive thousands of customer reviews every month. Feedback typically covers delivery speed, food quality, and customer support experience.

Positive Feedback

  • Many customers appreciate fast delivery times, often arriving quicker than the estimated time shown in the app.
  • Zomato's review system is praised for going deeper into the overall dining experience, not just delivery speed.
  • Users highlight simple and effective app interfaces that make ordering and booking easy.

Common Problems Faced by Customers

  • Delivery Delays - Orders showing 25-30 minutes delivery time often taking over an hour, causing frustration especially during dinner time.
  • Poor Refund Process - Customers report slow or unhelpful responses when requesting refunds for cancelled or wrong orders.
  • Customer Support Issues - Many users mention chatbot support being unhelpful, with no direct resolution to complaints.
  • Order Accuracy Problems - Missing items, wrong items delivered, or damaged/spilled food packaging upon arrival.
  • Delivery Verification Issues - Some customers report orders marked as "delivered" without proper OTP verification or confirmation call.
  • Hidden Charges - Extra payment requested for item replacements, sometimes removing applied coupon codes in the process.

Facilities Offered

  • Real-time Order Tracking - Customers can track their delivery partner's location live on the map.
  • Star-based Rating System -Users can rate restaurants and delivery service separately after each order.
  • Multiple Payment Options- UPI, cards, wallets, and cash on delivery supported.
  • Wide Restaurant Network -Access to thousands of restaurants across major Indian cities.
  • Quick Commerce (Zepto) -Grocery and essential items delivered within 10-15 minutes.
  • In-app Chat Support- Chatbot and support ticket system for order-related issues.
  • Offers & Discounts - Regular coupon codes and membership plans (like Swiggy One) for reduced delivery fees.

Overall Rating Trend: Customer ratings tend to focus heavily on delivery reliability and support responsiveness, showing that speed and service consistency remain key factors in user satisfaction.
