Service Based Company


Service-based IT companies provide software development, consulting, and technical support services to other businesses across the globe, rather than selling their own products. These companies work on client projects, handling everything from software maintenance to large-scale digital transformation. In India, companies like TCS (Tata Consultancy Services), Infosys, Tech Mahindra, and Deloitte are leading examples of service-based organizations. TCS and Infosys are among the largest IT service providers in the world, offering solutions in cloud computing, cybersecurity, and enterprise software to clients across various industries. Tech Mahindra specializes in telecom and digital services, while Deloitte offers consulting, auditing, and technology advisory services. Service-based companies typically hire in large numbers and are a popular starting point for freshers entering the IT industry.

Product Based Company

Product-based IT companies design, build, and sell their own software products or platforms, rather than working on client projects. These companies invest heavily in research and development to create innovative solutions used by millions of people and businesses worldwide. In India, companies like Zoho, Freshworks, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and Flipkart are prime examples of product-based organizations. Zoho and Freshworks are homegrown SaaS (Software as a Service) companies offering business tools like CRM, email, and customer support platforms used globally. Google and Microsoft are global tech giants with widely used products such as search engines, operating systems, and cloud services. Amazon combines its e-commerce platform with AWS, a leading cloud computing service, while Flipkart operates one of India's largest e-commerce platforms. Product-based companies usually offer strong technical exposure, ownership of features, and are known for competitive salaries in the IT industry.
