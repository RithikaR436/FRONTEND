Schools in Orathanadu Taluk

Orathanadu is home to a number of schools that provide primary, secondary, and higher secondary education...

LKMH School

LKMH School, located on Mannargudi Road, Orathanadu, offers education from Grades 1 to 12 under the State Board.

Little Rose Matriculation Higher Secondary School

Little Rose Matriculation Higher Secondary School, established in 1997, is one of the well-known private educational institutions in Orathanadu. Located in Orathanadu (Ward-13), the school follows the State Board syllabus with English as the primary medium of instruction. It is a co-educational school offering classes from 1 to 10, along with an attached pre-primary section.
