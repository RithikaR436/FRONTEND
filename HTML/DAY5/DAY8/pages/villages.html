Villages in Orathanadu Taluk

Orathanadu taluk comprises numerous villages that form the backbone of its rural economy. Villages like Nadur and Thoppuviduthy are among the most populated in the taluk.Orathanadu Taluk in Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu has 118 Census 2011 village records, including 117 inhabited villages. The listed villages cover 236,624 people, 58,776 households and 56,851.25 hectares. Reported local context includes 81 Gram Panchayats, village facilities, land-use fields, nearest-town references and individual village profiles. Census datasets record this administrative level as a sub-district, while local usage may call it a tehsil, taluka, mandal, block or tahasil.
