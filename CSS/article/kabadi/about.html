The Revolution

Redefining India's Ancient Sport

Launched in 2014 by Mashal Sports and Star Sports, the Pro Kabaddi League (PKL) revolutionized an age-old indigenous Indian sport into a world-class television spectacle.

By combining electrifying arena lighting, international players from Iran, South Korea, Kenya, and Japan, cutting-edge video reviews, and franchise loyalty, PKL is now the second most-watched sporting league in India after cricket.

Official Rules of Pro Kabaddi

Understanding the core regulations that create thrilling 40-minute mat wars.

30-Second Raid Clock

Each raider has exactly 30 seconds to cross the baulk line, attempt a touch on defenders, and safely return across the mid-line. Failure results in an out.

Baulk Line & Bonus Line

The raider must cross the baulk line for a valid raid. If there are 6 or 7 defenders on court, crossing the bonus line with one foot in the air earns an additional 1 bonus point.

Do-or-Die Raid Rule

If a team executes two successive empty raids (no points scored), the 3rd raid becomes a 'Do-or-Die' raid. The raider MUST score a point or be ruled OUT automatically!

Super Tackle (2 Points)

When a defending team has 3 or fewer players remaining on court and manages to successfully tackle the incoming raider, they are rewarded 2 points instead of 1.

Super Raid (3+ Points)

When a single raider scores 3 or more raid points (touch points plus bonus) in a single raid, it is celebrated as a game-changing Super Raid.

All-Out / Lona (2 Points)

When an entire team's 7 players are eliminated, an All-Out (Lona) is declared. The opposing team receives 2 bonus points, and all 7 dismissed players revive.
